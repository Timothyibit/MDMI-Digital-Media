Privacy Policy for MDMI Devotionals

Effective Date: November 9, 2026

The Master's Discipleship Ministries Inc. ("MDMI", "we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting your privacy and spiritual journey. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when using the MDMI Devotionals mobile application (Package ID: org.mdmi.devotionals) and associated services.

1. Information We Collect

MDMI Devotionals is built with privacy-first principles. We collect only what is essential for application functionality:

  • Account Information: If you choose to sign in via Google Authentication, we receive your name and email address provided by Google Identity Services to sync your personal bookmarks, journal entries, and devotional reflections across devices.
  • Spiritual Journal & Bookmarks: Your personal prayer points, notes, and reading reflections are stored privately in your personal account partition in Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Device Push Tokens: With your explicit consent, we register an anonymous Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push token to deliver scheduled prayer watches, morning devotional alerts, and ministry announcements.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data.
  • We do not serve third-party commercial advertisements.
  • We do not track your physical location (GPS coordinates).
  • We do not collect financial, health, or sensitive demographic information.

3. Audio Narration & AI Services

Our audio narration feature uses Google Gemini API to synthesize spoken devotionals. No personal user data or listening habits are shared with external AI training systems.

4. Data Storage and Security

All data is transmitted securely using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS) encryption. User profiles and private notes are protected by Google Firebase Firestore security rules, ensuring only authenticated users can access their own private journal entries.

5. Data Deletion and User Rights

You have full ownership of your data. You may delete individual journal entries, reading logs, and saved bookmarks at any time from within the app. To request permanent deletion of your account and all associated cloud records, contact our ministry administration at themastersmdmi.dev@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

MDMI Devotionals is designed for general audiences (13+) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
